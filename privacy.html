CHESS EMPIRES — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: September 2026

Chess Empires is a game by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

WHAT THE APP COLLECTS
Nothing on its own. Chess Empires has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Your campaign saves and settings stay on your device.

GAME CENTER
Multiplayer matches use Apple's Game Center. If you sign in, Apple provides your Game Center display name to the app and to your opponent so matches can be shown, and Apple stores match data on its servers. Apple's handling of that data is described in Apple's privacy policy. The app does not copy Game Center data anywhere else.

PURCHASES
In-app purchases, when offered, are processed by Apple. The app receives only a confirmation of what was purchased so it can unlock the content; it never sees your payment details.

CHILDREN
The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
